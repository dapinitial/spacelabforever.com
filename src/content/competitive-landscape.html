The posture · what we do & don't ship

Public protocols only. None of Apple's proprietary code.

Standard protocols, fresh implementations.

SuperAudio talks to speakers over their own published protocols — AirPlay 1 (RAOP) and Sonos (UPnP) — with our own from-scratch Swift, not lifted GPL or proprietary code. Same posture Airfoil and AirConnect have operated under for years.

We built a full AirPlay 2 sender too, and verified it against real Apple hardware — but Apple's FairPlay DRM gates the audio on modern receivers, so we've parked it rather than ship something that doesn't play. Modern Sonos is unaffected (it uses Sonos' own protocol); HomePods are the one speaker this keeps out for now.
